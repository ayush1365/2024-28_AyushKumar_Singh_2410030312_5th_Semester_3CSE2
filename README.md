## 🔄 Data Science Workflow

The internship followed a sequential data science workflow:

```text
Data Input
    ↓
Data Cleaning
    ↓
Exploratory Data Analysis
    ↓
Feature Preparation
    ↓
Model Training
    ↓
Evaluation
    ↓
Interpretation & Reporting
```

The workflow begins with raw or collected data, followed by cleaning and preparation. Exploratory analysis is then performed to identify patterns and relationships. Suitable features are prepared for modelling, after which a machine-learning model may be trained and evaluated. Finally, the results are interpreted and communicated through tables, graphs and written observations.

---

## 📚 Methodology

The internship methodology consisted of the following stages:

1. **Problem Understanding** – Define the objective, identify the required data and determine the expected output or insight.
2. **Data Collection** – Obtain relevant datasets and understand the meaning of the variables.
3. **Data Pre-processing** – Inspect the data, handle missing or inconsistent values, remove unnecessary information and prepare the dataset.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis** – Use descriptive statistics and visualisation to understand distributions, relationships, trends and anomalies.
5. **Feature Preparation** – Select useful variables and transform the data into a suitable form.
6. **Model Development** – Apply an appropriate machine-learning approach when prediction or classification is required.
7. **Evaluation** – Assess the analytical or model output using suitable evaluation measures.
8. **Interpretation and Reporting** – Convert technical findings into clear observations and document the process, limitations and outcomes.
